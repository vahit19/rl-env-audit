# Prime Intellect Environments Hub, 13 October 2026

The 60 most-starred public environments tagged `single-turn`, each installed in its own virtualenv and scored with its own grader (`--n 60`).

- **21 audited**: 19 passed every check; 2 paid at least one check in full without a correct answer (kept anonymous here until their authors have been told privately and have had time to fix them).
- **39 not auditable** by these checks: 16 could not be loaded or scored offline in this setup (needs credentials or a gated dataset, or does not import with verifiers 0.4.0); 14 no single reference answer the reward reliably checks (instruction following, similarity, open-ended scoring); 6 tasks carry no reference answer field; 2 could not be installed; 1 written against an interim verifiers API.

## Not auditable by these checks

The names of the 39 environments that could not be audited are withheld, so that the two failing environments cannot be identified by elimination. The reasons are counted above; none of them is a finding about the environment's grader.

## Fixes

Both failures have a small fix, written and checked with this tool on 14 October 2026; the patches go to the authors first.

| Environment | Check | Before the fix | After the fix | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Environment A | `hedge_or` | 466/500 | 0/500 | accept containment only when the reference answer closes the reply, and never for a reply that hedges with "or", "either", "maybe" or "/" |
| Environment A | `candidate_list` | 500/500 | 0/500 | (same change) |
| Environment B | `echo_prompt` | 11/299 | 0/299 | extract the boxed answer in strict mode, so a reply without `\boxed{}` is not verified as a whole |

For Environment A the fix was also checked on honest replies: ten common phrasings of the correct answer (5,000 replies) are paid exactly as before.
